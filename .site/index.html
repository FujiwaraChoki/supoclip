Long Twitch and Kick videos download only the parts clips need

The step that downloads selected segments fetched the whole video, then cut out the clips. For a one-hour VOD the download could never finish in time. It now fetches only the pieces under the clips.

What gets downloaded

Twitch serves a VOD as a playlist of 10-second pieces. A 59-minute 1080p60 VOD has 356 of them. These are the pieces the test run needed for its two clips.

~3.3 GB
Per-job limit
2 GB
Only the clips

What happened

One customer's 59-minute Twitch VOD failed three times with "Stream download timed out". Times are UTC.

  1. 11:32 → 11:37

    Attempt 1. The audio download was slow but still receiving data. It was stopped at exactly 300 seconds.

  2. 11:39 → 11:47

    Attempt 2. Audio finished in about 2 minutes, then the transcript and AI analysis picked 12 clips. Downloading the selected segments started fetching the whole 3.3 GB VOD and was stopped at 300 seconds.

  3. 11:51 → 11:56

    Attempt 3. Same again. yt-dlp estimated 55 minutes left when it was stopped.

  4. 12:17

    Fix deployed to the render worker after it finished its last running job. No job was interrupted.

Two causes

The whole video was downloaded

The selected time ranges were dropped, so the entire VOD was fetched through the proxy (~1 MiB/s) before any cutting. A one-hour 1080p60 VOD is also larger than the 2 GB per-job limit, so more time alone would not have helped.

A fixed 300-second wall clock

Every stream download had to finish within 300 seconds in total, even while data was still arriving. At proxy speeds that is about 300 MB.

What changed

  • ✓
    Only the needed pieces. SupoClip reads the playlist, picks the pieces under each padded clip range and downloads them four at a time. The pieces count against the same 2 GB limit and can still be cancelled, then the clips are cut locally as before.
  • ✓
    Safe fallback. Encrypted, byte-range or discontinuous playlists fall back to downloading the whole video, as before.
  • ✓
    A timeout that tracks progress. YOUTUBE_PROXY_TIMEOUT_SECONDS now means 300 seconds without receiving data. The new STREAM_DOWNLOAD_MAX_SECONDS caps one download at 40 minutes. YouTube downloads are unchanged.
  • ✓
    Hotfixes recovered into git. The 13 November bandwidth fix and proxy trial were running in production but had never been committed. The PR's first commit adds them exactly as deployed.

How it was checked

  • ✓
    New tests run real yt-dlp and ffmpeg against a local test stream. They cover which pieces are requested, timestamps matching the source, shared pieces between overlapping clips, fallbacks, the byte limit and both timeouts.
  • ✓
    The full backend suite passed: 3181 passed, 2 skipped.
  • ✓
    A run against the failing VOD over a direct connection produced the expected 81 seconds of 1080p video with audio, with timestamps matching the source.
  • ✓
    The same tests pass inside the production image. The worker started with no errors or restarts.
