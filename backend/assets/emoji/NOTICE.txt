Twemoji 72x72 PNG assets
========================

Source:  https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji (actively-maintained fork of the
         original Twitter Twemoji project)
Version: v17.0.3
Files:   one PNG per codepoint (filename = lowercase hex codepoint, variation
         selectors stripped, matching Twemoji's own asset naming convention),
         covering exactly the emoji offered by the reaction picker
         (frontend/src/components/ui/emoji-picker.tsx's REACTION_EMOJIS).

Used to burn true-color emoji reactions into exported clips via ffmpeg's
`overlay` filter (see backend/src/emoji_reactions.py) — ffmpeg's ASS/
subtitles filter cannot composite full-color glyphs (COLR or bitmap/CBDT)
from a font in this project's ffmpeg build, so reactions are rendered as
image overlays instead of text glyphs.
