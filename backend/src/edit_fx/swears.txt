# so every word here is bleeped when the user's censor setting is on, even when the model missed it.

# Format: one entry per line, lowercase, accents kept. `#` starts a comment.
#   word      matches a transcript word whose normalised form (lowercase, letters and digits only) is exactly `word`
#   stem*     matches any normalised word that starts with `stem`
#   two words matches that exact word sequence (each word normalised)
#
# Keep it to strong profanity and slurs. Mild words (damn, hell, crap) are not bleeped, and words that are
# ordinary vocabulary in another listed language are left out on purpose (Spanish "con", Indonesian "anjing",
# Malay "suka", Spanish "concha"/"polla"/"hostia") so a bleep never lands on an innocent word.
