SupoClip dynamic-edit built-in assets
===================================

Everything in this directory is committed, so it ships in the Docker image (COPY src/) and the docker-compose bind
mount, like src/media/models/. The music beds and the emoji atlas are generated by code in backend/scripts/fx/
(`make fx-assets`, needs ffmpeg, deno and the Noto Color Emoji font); the sound effects are curated recordings.
CI never regenerates assets, it only checks that manifest.json's sha256 values match the files. manifest.json lists
every file with its hash, duration and levels (written by backend/scripts/fx/write_manifest.py).

sfx/*.wav  -  CC0-1.0 (public domain dedication); per-file provenance in SOURCES-CC0.txt
    74 sound effects, 4-8 takes for each sound id (whoosh impact pop riser record_scratch boom cash_register ticker
    slap stamp glitch tape_stop), edited from Freesound.org uploads that their authors released under CC0 1.0.
    The licence of every source was read on its Freesound page on 2026-11-14. SOURCES-CC0.txt lists each file
    with its Freesound URL, author and original title, and the processing applied. CC0 needs no attribution;
    the authors are listed for provenance and thanks.
    Format: 48 kHz 16-bit PCM WAV, mono or stereo as recorded, sample peak -6 dBFS, at most 2.5 s, named
    sfx/<sound>-<name>.wav. Risers are exactly 1.5 s or 2.5 s and end on their peak; variant numbers (the order in
    write_manifest.py SFX_FILES) alternate them, so riser variant v is 1.5 s when v is even and 2.5 s when odd.
    The bleep is not a file: it is a 1 kHz tone generated by the mixer (media/fx_audio.py).
    backend/scripts/fx/synth_sfx.py still holds the code-only (synthesised) set these replaced; it writes them as
    FX_LIBRARY_DIR drop-ins for anyone who wants sounds made entirely from code.

music/*.m4a  -  CC0-1.0 (public domain dedication)
    Composed and synthesised by backend/scripts/fx/synth_music.py: a numpy step sequencer with sine kicks,
    noise snares and hats, saturated sine bass, detuned saw pads and additive plucks, original progressions
    and melodies. 32 bars per mood, rendered circularly so each file loops seamlessly, normalised to about
    -20 LUFS, AAC 128 kbit/s stereo 48 kHz.
    hype (124 bpm, A minor), chill (82, maj7 lofi), dramatic (70, D minor), funny (112, C major),
    inspiring (96, D major), dark (88, E phrygian)

emoji/atlas.png, emoji/atlas.json  -  SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see LICENSE-noto-emoji.txt)
    Glyph images rendered by backend/scripts/fx/build_emoji_atlas.ts from Noto Color Emoji
    (Copyright 2022 Google Inc., https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji; version in emoji/font.txt and
    manifest.json). 16 columns of 160 px cells; keys are the emoji strings (backend/scripts/fx/emoji_list.py:
    TONE_EMOJI, LLM_ALLOWED_EMOJI and EMOJI_KEYWORD_MAP values).
    The bitcoin sign (U+20BF) is not in the emoji font; its cell is a coin badge drawn with Inter
    (backend/fonts/Inter.ttf, SIL OFL 1.1, see backend/fonts/SOURCES.md).

Self-hosters can add or replace sounds and beds without touching this directory: drop files into
FX_LIBRARY_DIR (default backend/fx_library, /app/fx_library in the image), see backend/fx_library/README.txt.
Drop-ins are used at mix time only and never change which effects a clip gets.
